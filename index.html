Layout editor

Case in a Nutshell · phase 3

Click an object to select it, drag to move it (snaps to the grid, Shift = fine), R turns it, Delete removes it, arrow keys nudge. Cyan = free (place and look are yours), magenta = locked (a puzzle, a lock or a zoom view: place and facing only), grey = flat on the floor, red = a warning. Yellow marks are small items: they sit on a host and follow it.
